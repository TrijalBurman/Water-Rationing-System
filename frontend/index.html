SMALL CHANGES, SHARED BENEFITS

Make every drop count.

Simple habits help keep your community's supply healthy.

  • 01

    Fix the little drips

    Report a leaking tap or pipe when you spot it. Small, continuous losses add up.

  • 02

    Give the tap a break

    Turn off running water while brushing, shaving, or washing dishes.

  • 03

    Make a full load worthwhile

    Run washing machines with full loads and reuse suitable water for plants.

  • 04

    Keep an eye on your allowance

    Use your daily reading to understand your habits and plan around supply alerts.
